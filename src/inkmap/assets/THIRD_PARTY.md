# Third-party assets

## arm-left.json

A left arm cut from the MakeHuman base mesh (`makehuman/data/3dobjs/base.obj`,
https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman). The MakeHuman project released that
mesh under CC0 1.0 Universal in September 2020. `scripts/extract_arm.py` cuts the arm
out, thickens it, smooths it and adds the sleeve coordinates. The result is also offered
under CC0 1.0.
